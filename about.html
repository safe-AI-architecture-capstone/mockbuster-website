OUR WORK

One shared objective.

Demonstrate how AI agents can access useful business information without unrestricted access to a protected source database.

Our capstone combines database architecture, API development, role-based authorization, AI integration, and security testing into a proof-of-concept reporting system.

The project uses a proxy-first approach and a production-style dataset to examine the benefits and limitations of controlled AI access.
